## Welcome to the Data-Driven Fluid Dynamics & Aerodynamics Lab

We are a joint aerospace research group operating across the **National Institute for Aerospace Technology (INTA)** and **Universidad Carlos III de Madrid (UC3M)**.

Our research bridges classical **computational fluid dynamics (CFD)** and **high-fidelity experimental aerodynamics** with **modern machine learning architectures** ($\beta$-VAEs, generative models, and transformers) to develop real-time aerodynamic surrogates, flow super-resolution techniques, and active flow control systems.

---

### Core Research Pillars

1. **Nonlinear Reduced-Order Modeling (ROM):** Encoding complex spatio-temporal turbulent coherent structures into compact latent manifolds via $\beta$-Variational Autoencoders and predicting multi-scale dynamics with Transformers.
2. **Data-Driven Active Flow Control (AFC):** Designing closed-loop and model-predictive control (MPC) frameworks for aerodynamic drag reduction and unsteady wake manipulation.
3. **Flow Super-Resolution & Diagnostics:** Reconstructing high-resolution velocity and pressure fields from sparse sensor arrays and optical measurement techniques.
4. **Boundary Layers & Wall Turbulence:** High-Reynolds-number experimental and numerical analysis of turbulent boundary layers subject to adverse pressure gradients (APG).
