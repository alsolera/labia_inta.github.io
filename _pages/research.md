### 1. $\beta$-VAEs & Transformers for Fluid Dynamics ROMs
We design non-linear reduced-order models that outperform classical modal decompositions (such as POD/DMD). By combining disentangled representations from $\beta$-Variational Autoencoders with autoregressive Transformers, we capture both high-energy large scales and dissipative non-linear dynamics with minimal degrees of freedom.

### 2. Active Flow Control & Wake Stabilization
Targeting fuel-efficiency gains and drag mitigation in bluff bodies and aerodynamic platform components, our group implements active flow control strategies combining high-frequency pulsed micro-jets with Model Predictive Control (MPC) and reinforcement learning algorithms.

### 3. Super-Resolution Generative Models
Developing physics-informed and generative neural networks capable of upsampling sparsely sampled physical flow fields (from Particle Tracking Velocimetry and sparse surface pressure taps) into fully resolved, 3D volumetric turbulent flow representations.

### 4. Experimental Aerodynamics & Wind Tunnel Testing
Utilizing INTA’s wind tunnel facilities and UC3M aerodynamic laboratories to conduct high-fidelity PIV, PTV, and hot-wire measurements on non-equilibrium boundary layers, adverse pressure gradient airfoils, and full-scale ground transportation models.
