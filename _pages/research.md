### 1. $\beta$-VAEs & Transformers for Fluid Dynamics ROMs
We design non-linear reduced-order models that outperform classical modal decompositions (such as POD/DMD). By combining disentangled representations from $\beta$-Variational Autoencoders with autoregressive Transformers, we capture both high-energy large scales and dissipative non-linear dynamics with minimal degrees of freedom.

### 2. Active Flow Control & Wake Stabilization
Targeting fuel-efficiency gains and drag mitigation in bluff bodies and ground-transportation aerodynamics, our group implements active flow control strategies combining pulsed micro-jets with Model Predictive Control (MPC) and genetic-algorithm-based optimization.

### 3. Sparse-Sensor Flow Reconstruction & Super-Resolution
Developing generative neural networks and data-driven models capable of reconstructing full-resolution flow fields from sparse, non-intrusive measurements — including randomly-seeded PIV/PTV data and wall-mounted heat-transfer sensors — without requiring full-field high-resolution data for training.

### 4. Experimental Aerodynamics & Wind Tunnel Testing
Utilizing INTA's wind tunnel facilities and UC3M aerodynamic laboratories to conduct high-fidelity PIV, PTV, infrared thermography, and hot-wire measurements on non-equilibrium boundary layers, adverse pressure gradient flows, impinging jets, and full-scale ground transportation models.
